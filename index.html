About Horizon Pilot

Horizon Pilot is a 3D flying game that runs in a phone browser. You start as a Cadet in a little trainer plane at Zaib International and work up to airliners and a jet fighter.

There are 25 career missions, 7 planes and pilot ranks, plus Free Flight and a Daily Challenge. You use the stick, throttle, gear and flaps to take off, fly through rings and land on the glide path.

3 quick tips

  1. To take off, go to full throttle, roll straight down the runway and pull back gently at take-off speed.
  2. To land, line up from far away, lower the gear and flaps, reduce throttle and follow the glide path dots.
  3. If you come in too fast or too steep, throttle up, climb away and try again.
